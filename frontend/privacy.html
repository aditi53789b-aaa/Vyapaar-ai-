Privacy Notice

Proposed effective date: 21 November 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Information this service handles

Depending on the features you use, BoloKhaata may process your name, email address, password hash, email-verification and security records, business type, shop name, approximate annual turnover range, state, district, preferred language, customer names, invoice details, sales, expenses, payment records and voice transcripts submitted for parsing.

2. Sensitive information

Do not enter Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, bank-account credentials, card details, passwords for other services or other sensitive identifiers into free-text fields. Account creation does not require these details. Any future document-upload feature must have a separate security, access-control, retention and consent review before it is enabled.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to create and secure your account, verify your email, maintain your business ledger, generate invoices, show dashboard summaries, process voice-entry drafts, respond to support requests and display relevant programme information. We should not use your records for unrelated advertising or sell them.

4. Voice processing

When you activate voice entry, your browser may use its speech-recognition service to produce a transcript. The transcript is sent to BoloKhaata to prepare a draft. Check the browser's own privacy terms and review the transcript before confirming any financial entry.

5. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure, database, email-delivery and hosting providers strictly to operate the service. The operator must identify the actual providers, their locations, contractual safeguards and any cross-border transfers here before launch. Information may also be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

6. Security

The application is designed to use password hashing, email verification, short-lived access tokens, tenant-scoped records and restricted cross-origin access. No internet service can promise perfect security. The operator must maintain access reviews, secure backups, dependency updates, monitoring and an incident-response process.

7. Retention and deletion

Account and business records should be retained only for the period needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. The operator must publish concrete retention periods and provide a working account export and deletion process before public launch. Deleting an account may require a documented treatment of invoices and records that must be retained by law.

8. Your choices and rights

You may contact the operator to request access, correction, export, deletion or other rights available under applicable law. The operator must publish a working contact channel and grievance process. You can stop using voice input at any time.

9. Children

The service is intended for business users and is not designed for children. The operator must define and enforce an appropriate minimum-age policy before launch.

10. Changes and contact

Material changes should be reflected in a versioned notice. Before publication, insert the operator's full legal name, registered address, privacy contact, grievance officer details, actual service-provider list and contact response timelines here.
